## Commitments listed in the statement

Keyboard navigation; colour contrast; screen reader compatibility where practical; heading hierarchy and semantic HTML; form labels; visible focus; informative image alt text; captions/transcripts where applicable; no colour-only meaning; responsive zoom/layouts.
