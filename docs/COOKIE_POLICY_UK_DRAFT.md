# Cookie Policy (UK Draft)

Status: Draft for legal/compliance review.

## Cookie Categories
- Essential cookies:
  - Session authentication and security state.
  - Parent profile/PIN gating continuity.
- Functional cookies:
  - Preference continuity and UX state where applicable.
- Analytics/diagnostic cookies:
  - Operational diagnostics, performance and reliability telemetry where enabled.

## Current Controls
- Authentication cookies are httpOnly and security-configured.
- Parent consent and access checks occur server-side and client-side bootstrapping.

## Parent Transparency
- Cookie policy link is included in AI disclosure and policy references.
- Detailed consent banner behavior and optional cookie controls require product/legal finalisation.
