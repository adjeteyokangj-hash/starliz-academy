# Safeguarding Policy (UK Draft)

Status: Draft for safeguarding lead and legal review.

## Safeguarding Objectives
- Protect child welfare while using learning services.
- Restrict high-risk access to sensitive records.
- Preserve evidence trails for incidents and interventions.

## Product Controls
- Role-based access and route guards for school/admin operations.
- Audit logs for sensitive events.
- Safeguarding alert and incident pathways for school-enabled deployments.

## Data Handling
- Safeguarding records are treated as sensitive data.
- Deletion and retention exceptions may apply under safeguarding obligations.

## Escalation and Governance
- Escalation workflows must include accountable roles and timeline records.
- Manual response playbooks required for high-severity incidents.

## Manual Review Required
- DSL and deputy DSL role accountability wording.
- Statutory retention requirements for safeguarding evidence.
- Incident notification timelines and external reporting rules.
