# Incident Response

Status: Draft runbook for launch operations.

## Incident Types
- Security incident.
- Child-data concern.
- Failed payment or webhook incident.
- Failed homework/job incident.
- Rollback incident.

## Initial Actions
1. Open incident channel and assign lead.
2. Capture scope and impact.
3. Preserve logs and timeline evidence.
4. Apply containment without destructive operations.

## Communication
- Parent/school communication must be factual and clear.
- Avoid exposing internal secrets or unverified details.
- Provide next update time in all active incidents.

## Escalation
- Security/child-data incidents escalate immediately to security and safeguarding owners.
- Payment incidents escalate to billing owner and support lead.
- Job incidents escalate to platform/on-call owner.

## Closure
- Confirm recovery.
- Publish internal incident summary.
- Track remediation actions.
