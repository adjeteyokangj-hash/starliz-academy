# AI Use Disclosure (UK Draft)

Status: Product transparency draft.
Legal review: Required before final public policy publication.

## How AI Is Used
- Adaptive lesson sequencing.
- Weak-area pattern detection.
- Parent progress insight generation.
- Content safety and moderation signals.

## What AI Is Not Used For
- Selling child data.
- Unsupervised legal or safeguarding decision making.
- Automated high-impact decisions without human oversight.

## Parent Controls
- Parent consent and withdrawal controls.
- Child profile management.
- Export and deletion request workflows.
- Parent-facing visibility of AI disclosure summary in consent workflows.

## Safeguards
- Role-based access controls.
- Audit logging for sensitive data actions.
- Policy-linked disclosures in parent/admin workflows.

## Important Limitations
- This disclosure is an engineering transparency statement and not legal advice.
- Final legal wording and policy cross-references must be approved by legal/privacy counsel.
