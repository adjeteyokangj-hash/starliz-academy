## Locked commercial position

| Area | Decision |
|---|---|
| Ordinary acknowledgement | Within **2 working days** of receipt |
| Ordinary substantive response | Within **10 working days** of receipt |
| Complex / escalated | Within **20 working days** (interim update by day 10 if still open) |
| Urgent access / payment-blocking | Acknowledge within **1 working day** |
| Working days | Monday–Friday, excluding UK bank holidays |
| Safeguarding | **Outside** ordinary complaint SLAs — escalate immediately |
| School-controlled Day School | Acknowledge and explain routing to the partner school |
| Escalation | Support → Platform Admin |
| Legal note | Targets, not guaranteed remedies — Draft for legal review |
