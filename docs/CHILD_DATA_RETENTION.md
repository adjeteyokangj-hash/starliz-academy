# Child Data Retention (UK Draft)

Status: Operational draft.
Legal review: Required before externally binding publication.

## Principles
- Keep only data needed for educational delivery, safeguarding, compliance, and reporting.
- Archive or remove data when no longer required for the purpose.
- Maintain audit trace for sensitive actions.

## Draft Retention Buckets
- Active learning records:
  - Data: attempts, assignments, progress and weak-area signals.
  - Retention target: while account is active plus defined post-closure retention period under legal review.
- Consent records:
  - Data: consent accepted/withdrawn timestamps and versions, consent audit events.
  - Retention target: retained for accountability and legal evidence requirements.
- Export/deletion request logs:
  - Data: request metadata, actor, timestamps, status trail.
  - Retention target: retained for compliance proof and dispute handling period.
- Safeguarding records:
  - Data: alerts/incidents and interventions where applicable.
  - Retention target: longer retention window subject to statutory safeguarding obligations.

## Current Product Enforcement Notes
- Parent consent is checked before child learning access.
- Parent export and deletion requests are tracked in auditable logs.
- Sensitive admin actions are logged for forensic review.

## Manual Review Required
- Final numeric retention periods by category.
- Legal hold and exception rules.
- Purge and anonymisation runbook sign-off.
