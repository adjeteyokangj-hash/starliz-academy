# Student Anus Architecture

## Purpose

Anus is the lifecycle governance layer for student learning artifacts in StarLiz Academy.

It defines what data is permanent, long-term, medium-term, short-term, or disposable, without executing destructive purge behavior in this phase.

## Boundary

- Brain thinks and exposes canonical read intelligence.
- Blood transports events and approved fields.
- Stomach digests evidence into normalized signals.
- Anus governs retention, archive eligibility, and disposal policy.

## Lifecycle categories

- permanent
- long_term
- medium_term
- short_term
- disposable

## Policy rules

- Permanent records remain retained with archive eligibility.
- Long-term records retain historical progression and exam intelligence.
- Medium-term records retain operational support history.
- Short-term records retain temporary workflow artifacts.
- Disposable records are limited to temporary or regenerable artifacts.

## This phase safety rules

- Automatic purge is disabled for every lifecycle category.
- Disposal policy exists only as a contract decision.
- Any future purge workflow must be explicit, supervised, and separately approved.

## Information flow

1. Brain and Stomach produce intelligence artifacts.
2. Blood transports those artifacts and events.
3. Anus classifies each artifact with deterministic lifecycle policy.
4. Archive/disposal decisions are exposed for controlled workflows.
5. No automatic deletion is performed by the Anus foundation.
