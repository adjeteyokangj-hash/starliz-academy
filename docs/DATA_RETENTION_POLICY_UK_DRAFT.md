# Data Retention Policy (UK Draft)

Status: Draft for legal, safeguarding, and operations review.

## Retention Policy Goals
- Minimise stored personal data.
- Retain evidence needed for legal and safeguarding obligations.
- Enforce traceable deletion and archival workflows.

## Data Classes (Draft)
- Identity and account records.
- Child profile and learning records.
- Consent and policy acceptance records.
- Billing and transaction records.
- Safeguarding records and incident logs.
- Audit logs for sensitive actions.

## Operational Controls
- Consent and deletion/export actions are auditable.
- Parent-owned child data request workflow logs request status.
- Sensitive admin deletion actions are explicitly logged.

## Deletion and Erasure Workflow
- Parent requests are tracked as request events.
- Fulfilment requires policy-governed execution and verification.
- Safeguarding and legal-hold exceptions must be reviewed before final deletion.

## Manual Review Required
- Final retention schedule by table/dataset.
- Anonymisation standards and irreversible-delete criteria.
- Legal hold override process.
