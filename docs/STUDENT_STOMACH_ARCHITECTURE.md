# Student Stomach Architecture

## Purpose

Stomach is the digestion layer for learner evidence in StarLiz Academy.

It converts raw events into normalized learning signals that other organs can safely consume.

## Boundary

- Brain thinks and reads canonical intelligence.
- Blood transports events and approved fields.
- Stomach digests raw evidence into typed signals.
- Anus applies lifecycle retention and disposal policy.

Stomach does not replace Brain or Blood. It prepares evidence for Brain and transport contracts.

## Inputs

Stomach accepts typed evidence envelopes such as:

- lesson completion
- questions answered
- homework submissions
- quick level finder completion
- reading and spelling activity
- assessment and exam outcomes
- coach support usage
- competition outcomes

## Outputs

Stomach produces deterministic digested signals, for example:

- mastery signals
- confidence signals
- weak-area signals
- support signals
- intervention signals
- readiness signals
- acceleration signals
- engagement signals

## What belongs in Stomach

- Evidence typing and validation
- Deterministic evidence-to-signal digestion
- Confidence shaping from evidence payload data
- Pure transformation contracts for downstream organs

## What must not go in Stomach

- Database writes
- Route handlers or UI rendering
- Payment or access policy decisions
- Lifecycle deletion and purge operations

## Information flow

1. Blood receives event evidence and routes it.
2. Stomach digests event evidence into signals.
3. Brain reads digested signals as part of canonical intelligence views.
4. Blood transports resulting intelligence to approved destinations.
5. Anus governs retention/archive/disposal policy for stored artifacts.
