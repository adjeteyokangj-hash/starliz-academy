# Privacy Policy (UK Draft)

Status: Draft for legal and policy review.
Not legal advice.

## 1. Who We Are
StarLiz Academy provides parent-managed digital learning services for children.

## 2. Data We Collect
- Parent account and contact details.
- Child profile and learning progress records.
- Consent and audit records.
- Subscription and payment-related metadata.

## 3. Why We Process Data
- Provide educational services.
- Personalise learning pathways.
- Support account security and billing.
- Meet legal, safeguarding, and compliance obligations.

## 4. Parent Consent and Child Data
- Parent/guardian consent is required for child learning access.
- Consent acceptance and withdrawal are timestamped and auditable.
- Parents can withdraw consent and request data actions.

## 5. AI Processing Disclosure
- AI supports adaptive learning and recommendations.
- AI disclosure is surfaced in parent consent workflows.
- AI use is bounded by safeguarding and governance controls.

## 6. Parent Rights
- Access and export requests.
- Deletion/erasure requests.
- Consent withdrawal.

## 7. Security and Governance
- Role-based access control.
- Audit logging of sensitive actions.
- Operational monitoring and incident response.

## 8. Data Retention
- Retention follows product policy and legal obligations.
- Final retention schedule requires legal sign-off.
